# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- **Light or dark, set once for every app:** press **L** in pca (or `pca look light`, `pca look dark`, `pca look auto`). usual's window follows it.

## 1.0.0
- usual keeps your details (name, phone, email, addresses), the people you order for, the places you order from, and your usual orders.
- Each usual prints as a receipt: what you get, the tax, delivery fee and tip, the total, and what each person owes.
- Order it three ways: online (the website, and every detail ready to copy), by phone (the number and exactly what to say), or by email (the whole order written out).
- A *Fill in my details* bookmark fills in checkout pages (it skips password boxes and never sends anything anywhere).
- **I ordered it** stamps the receipt and keeps it in your history, with what you spend each month and where.
- "It's Friday evening: you often get Friday pizza from Sal's around now."
- Reminders, and a Touch ID lock (Apple's own check: a photo can't fool it).
- `usual order`, `usual list`, `usual history` in Terminal.
- A street of food places along the top: painted signs by day, neon by night, a delivery rider going by, and Rocco the raccoon waiting by the bins.
